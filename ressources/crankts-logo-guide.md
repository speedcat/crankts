# CrankTS logo

The mark combines a **C-shaped flywheel** with an **upward crank handle**. Its cream brush lettering, heavy black contours, electric blue, and yellow paint swipe deliberately echo the energetic workshop-comic style of `crankts-illustration2.png`.

## Files

- `crankts-icon.svg` — compact square icon for GitHub avatars, favicons, and social profiles.
- `crankts-wordmark.svg` — self-contained horizontal lockup that stays readable on light and dark backgrounds.
- `crankts-icon.png` and `crankts-wordmark.png` — raster previews for quick use.

## Colors

- Electric TypeScript blue: `#18B9F2`
- Flywheel blue: `#197EBB`
- Workshop black: `#07090D`
- Safety yellow: `#FFC13D`
- Painted cream: `#FFF7E8`

## Editing

Every visual element is a named SVG group or path. The hand-painted lettering is drawn as paths rather than referencing a font, so it renders consistently without external assets.

Keep at least 12.5% of the icon width as clear space. For very small uses below 24 px, remove the two orange acceleration marks if they become visually crowded.
